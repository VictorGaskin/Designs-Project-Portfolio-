**Frame Design**

  *Material Considerations for Frame* 

  I consulted an Ashby Chart to determine that material, but I did not do it blindly. I knew that the material needed to do better than sustain the pressure on the system. The pulley system would generate a considerable amount of heat and friction, and I had to choose a material that could sustain both the pressure, generated heat, and frictional forces. A few times I almost chose a material like PM that is a glass substitute, this would have been catastrophic for a design in reality. 

I eventually chose PEEK as it is creep resistant, heat resistant, and pressure resistant.
