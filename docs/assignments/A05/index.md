# A5 – Strap Bracket Design

## Objective

The purpose of this project is to design a bracket that could support a strap delivering a uniform horizontal force. The design constraints that needed to be accounted for was material strength and stiffness. 

## Analyze

This project separated the whole bracket design into 5 individual components whose dimensions needed to be solved for in reference to the force distributed by the strap. 

Components A-E are listed below.

These components needed to have their dimensions solved for accounting for strength and stiffness, meaning there was ten steps needed to solve for the whole system comprehensively. The largest dimension was chosen as the governing dimension due to that dimension being able to satisfy both the strength and stiffness requirement. 

Before solving, the material for the strap bracket needed to be chosen. I chose Ti-6Al-4V titanium as the material of choice. I determined that this metal would be the best choice due to it being extremely lightweight while retaining a strength comparable if not higher than most conventional steels. 

The choice in material determined material properties that drove the design process moving forward. 

(The Modulus of Elasticity and Yield Strength was determined from this analysis paper: https://inldigitallibrary.inl.gov/content/uploads/50/2026/04/Sort_63131.pdf) 

I chose a force value of 650lbf. This fell between the ranges provided in the project given, and given the safety factor of 4, this bracket could sustain higher forces without undergoing plastic deformation.
